# Commission (module `commission`)

Staff and sales commission for the cashier (`kasir`), invoices (`crm`, `grosir_distribusi`) and the payslip
(`karyawan_gaji`). Rangka-apps#110. Added per site like `promotions`: `config.modules: {commission: ^1.0}` and the plan lists it.

## Flow
1. Head office writes **rules** (`commission_rule`): source (cashier receipt or sales invoice), what it covers (all, one
   category, one item), percent of the net line or a fixed amount per item, dates and a priority. The most specific live
   rule wins: item over category over all, then priority.
2. **Cashier**: every counter line may name who did the work ("Dikerjakan oleh", tap the line); the sale has a default
   staff ("Staf penjualan", in the folded "Sebelum bayar" box). A submitted receipt makes one `commission_line` per line a
   rule covers. **Invoices**: a regular invoice with a salesman (module `distribution`) does the same, on issue.
3. **Base**: the line's net amount after every discount (line, promo, bill, member, points) and before PPN.
   POS: `line.amount * (total - ppn_in_sales) / subtotal`; invoice: `line.amount * (subtotal - discount) / subtotal`
   (a down payment never lowers it). A free gift line earns nothing.
4. A line a rule covers without staff is kept as `no_staff` (amount 0); a named staff without a rule is `no_rule`. The
   dashboard `komisi_bulan_ini` counts both, so nothing is silently zero.
5. **Statement** (`commission_statement`): finance drafts one per employee and period, with the tax month it is paid in,
   and ticks "Kunci rekap". The lock takes every live, unlocked line of the employee dated up to the period's last day and fills the
   total. Ticking "Batal" releases the lines. (It is not a submittable document: a flow cannot write to a submitted one.)
6. A **void** of a sale: its lines are cancelled when no statement holds them yet. After the lock the line is marked
   `reversed` and a negative correction line dated the cancel day is made, which the next statement nets.
7. **Payslip**: the fill flows (`payroll_run_fill`, `payroll_run_fill_on_create`, `payslip_from_attendance`, generated by
   `scripts/gen_payroll_fill_flows.py`) put the employee's locked statements of the slip's tax month, net of corrections
   and never below 0, into `payslip.piece_income`. It is inside `cash_gross` (so inside `gross_taxable`, the PPh 21 base,
   PMK 168/2023) and outside `bpjs_base` (PP 44/2015, Perpres 82/2018: only the fixed wage). A hand-made slip is typed.

## Who sees what
Head office manages rules and reads lines; finance locks statements; cashiers and outlet staff only create lines through
the flows. Every employee reads only their own lines and statements (member grant `employee == user.name`), shown on the
`Saya` page as "Komisi bulan ini".

## Not in v1
A negative statement total (corrections of an already paid month) pays 0 on the slip and is not carried forward; returns and
credit notes do not reverse commission (only a cancel of the receipt or invoice does); one live statement per employee and tax
month and immutable statement fields after the lock are not enforced yet (finance ticks "Batal", which is refused once a
submitted payslip of the tax month exists); foreign-currency invoices are not commissioned.
Tiers by monthly target, team splits, commission on collected (paid) invoices, clawback after the slip is paid,
production piece wages in `piece_income` (a flow cannot sum child rows; needs a top-level mirror in `production`), and the
`payslip_from_recap` (client-run) path.
